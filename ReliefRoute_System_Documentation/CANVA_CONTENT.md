# Canva Content — ReliefRoute
## Brand
**RELIEFROUTE** — *Right Supplies. Right Place. Right Time.* (Working name; confirm with team.)

## One-line description
A disaster-relief coordination platform that matches community needs with available supplies and delivery capacity, then tracks allocations and deliveries through an explainable, human-reviewed workflow.

## Problem
During floods and other emergencies, requests for help, warehouse inventory, urgency and transport capacity may be scattered across different sources. Coordinators can struggle to identify urgent unmet needs and available supplies.

## Solution
One platform for registering requests, managing supplies, calculating transparent priorities, recommending allocations, reviewing decisions and tracking delivery.

## Users
Relief coordinators, warehouse managers, field operators/volunteers, administrators.

## Workflow
Register needs → record inventory/capacity → calculate priorities → recommend allocation using demand, stock and distance → human approval → track delivery → rerun when conditions change.

## Differentiators
Explainable score; supply-versus-demand visibility; distance-aware warehouse choice; unmet demand shown explicitly; human approval; audit trail; recommendations update after changes.

## MVP
Simulated requests, warehouses, stock, locations and capacity; real frontend/backend/database; authentication/roles; allocation engine; delivery workflow; tests and setup docs.

## Visual direction
Black-and-white handmade doodle style with restrained orange, blue, green and red accents. Sketch relief boxes, water bottles, trucks, shelters, map pins and volunteers. Keep data, forms and statuses clear and accessible.

## Demo story
A community requests urgent water. The nearest warehouse has limited stock. ReliefRoute explains priority, recommends what can be supplied, shows what remains unmet, and tracks delivery after approval. When stock or needs change, the next run updates the recommendation.

## Limitations
Prototype uses simulated data and simplified distance/capacity assumptions. It does not replace official emergency systems, verified road navigation or human decisions.
